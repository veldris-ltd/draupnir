## How it is demonstrated

The target is step time within one per cent of the same job run by hand. The Sindri estate does not exist yet: SAD 1.3 puts the hardware build in VLD-INF-SINDRI-001 and out of scope here. The control plane renders a plan and does not sit in the training loop, which is the design reason to expect it; expecting is not measuring.
